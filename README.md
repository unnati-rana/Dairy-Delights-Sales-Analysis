# Introduction
Dairy Delights is a dairy products company involved in the production and sale of a variety of dairy products. The company deals with different products, brands, farm locations, customer locations, and sales channels. The available dataset contains information related to dairy production, product prices, quantities sold, revenue, inventory, and customer locations.
This project focuses on analysing the sales data of Dairy Delights to understand the overall sales performance of the company. The analysis helps in identifying products with higher sales and revenue, understanding customer demand, comparing different sales channels, and analysing inventory levels. By using this data, Dairy Delights can identify important sales trends and make better data-driven business decisions.
# Objectives
•	To analyse the overall sales performance of Dairy Delights.
•	To identify the best-performing dairy products.
•	To analyse the quantity of products sold.
•	To identify products generating higher revenue.
•	To compare sales across different locations.
•	To analyse sales through different sales channels.
•	To understand customer demand and purchasing patterns.
•	To analyse current inventory and stock levels.
•	To identify products that may require reordering.
•	To support better sales and inventory management decisions.
# Dataset Description
The Dairy Delights dataset contains 4,325 records and 23 columns. It provides information about dairy farms, products, sales, revenue, customers, and inventory.
 
Column Name	Description
Location	Location of the dairy farm
Total Land Area (acres)	Total land area of the farm
Number of Cows	Number of cows present on the farm
Farm Size	Size category of the dairy farm
Date	Date associated with the record
Product ID	Unique identification number of the product
Product Name	Name of the dairy product
Brand	Brand of the dairy product
Quantity (liters/kg)	Quantity of the product
Price per Unit	Price of the product per unit
Total Value	Total value of the product
Quantity Sold (liters/kg)	Quantity of the product sold
Price per Unit (sold)	Selling price per unit
Approx. Total Revenue (INR)	Approximate revenue generated from sales
Customer Location	Location of the customer
Sales Channel	Channel through which the product was sold
Quantity in Stock (liters/kg)	Quantity of product currently available in stock
Minimum Stock Threshold	Minimum stock level to be maintained
Reorder Quantity	Quantity recommended for reordering
Production Date	Date on which the product was produced
Expiration Date	Date on which the product expires
Shelf Life (days)	Number of days the product remains usable
Storage Condition	Recommended storage condition for the product
The dataset can be used to analyse the relationship between production, sales, revenue, and inventory. It provides useful information for identifying high-demand products, understanding sales performance across different locations and channels, and monitoring stock levels.
Overall, the dataset provides a detailed view of the operations and sales activities of Dairy Delights and can be effectively used for sales analysis and business decision-making.
